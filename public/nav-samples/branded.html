Branded · Mega Nav Prototype

Move your Mountain.

This prototype demonstrates the canonical Branded family chrome. A 32px gray top bar carries the country selector, an 84px sticky frosted header carries the Phillips lockup with search and menu, and a 52px sticky sub-navigation pins beneath the header for in-page jumps. All three layers stack in z-order (50 / 40 / 30) and stay aligned to the 1240px container.

Top Bar

32px utility row in --gray-100 with US flag SVG and country selector. Sits above the main header on every Branded page.

Frosted Header

84px sticky header at z-index 50. Background rgba(255,255,255,0.92) with backdrop-filter: saturate(140%) blur(10px).

Sticky Sub-Nav

52px secondary navigation at z-index 40, sticky-pinned 84px from the top. The active link gets a 3px red underline.
